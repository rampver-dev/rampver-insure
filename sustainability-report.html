SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

RF INSURANCE BROKERS INC. (Rampver Insure or RFIBI)

RFIBI recognizes that Sustainability will always play a vital role in the operation of the company. The company realizes the need to focus on sustainability with impact on environmental, social and governance areas, with the latter being addressed thru its corporate governance practices.

One of the ways that RFIBI is achieving this objective of sustainability is through the company’s existing partnership and synergy, with insurance companies that have been in the business of providing sustainability standards and strategies. To push this further, the Company’s next step is to define targeted sustainability measures and to arrive at sustainability strategies.
