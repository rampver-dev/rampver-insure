CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS


RF INSURANCE BROKERS INC. (Rampver Insure or RFIBI)


I. RATIONALE

Every organization, whether it is a company, a club, a fraternal order or an association, has expectations of how its members should act among each other, as well as with those outside of the organization. The set of rules, values, and ethical principles that govern or cover these expectations is referred to as a Code of Conduct and Ethics or “Code of Conduct” or “Code”. These principles may be implied or written and they usually written for a clear reference.

A Code of Conduct further clarifies an organization's vision, mission, values and principles by linking them with standards of professional conduct. The Code conveys the values that the organization wishes to nurture in its officers and employees, and, by doing so, desired behavior are defined. Organizations may also impose penalties for breaking their Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct embodies the prescribed moral code for employees of RF Insurance Brokers Inc. (“Rampver Insure” or “RFIBI”). While the Code defines the offenses and the corresponding disciplinary sanctions that may be imposed, its overall purpose is to prevent any breach thereof rather than the imposition of the disciplinary measures.


II. SCOPE

The Code applies to all Rampver Insure employees, officers and directors, regardless of rank or employment status.

While this Code of Conduct states the specific disciplinary measure for a specific offense, it does not preclude Rampver Insure from imposing a lesser or greater penalty depending on the gravity of the offense committed. It is also without prejudice to existing Company policies on specific matters not covered herein and which provide for penalties in non-compliance therewith. In case of conflict between the provisions of this Code and existing Company policies on specific matters and procedure, the latter shall prevail.


III. OBJECTIVES

a. To provide the employees, officers and directors with clear standards and expectations of how to do their job or tasks by setting a detailed description of what is the most legal and ethical behavior expected of all employees, thereby enhancing the company’s core values and beliefs and which set the right culture for the Company.

b. To implement clear guidelines and standard of office conduct and procedures of its employees, officers and directors, which are to be followed faithfully by employees, officers and directors and this standard of conduct is not only acceptable to the Company and its employees, officers and directors but more particularly to its clients, partners and other third parties.


IV. POLICIES


A. STANDARDS OF CONDUCT

This Code sets forth the Standards of Conduct that are expected of all employees, officers and directors and enumerates/describes the acts or omissions regarded as violation of the prescribed norms of behavior of the Company or prejudicial to its interest.

1. CORPORATE PROPERTY AND INTERESTS

All employees, officers and directors shall exercise the highest degree of care in handling and using Corporate property and assets (which can be physical, technical, etc.) and shall protect it from loss or damage.

Corporate property and assets shall be used only for official business purposes. Its use for any private or personal purpose is strongly prohibited.

All employees, officers and directors are likewise expected to safeguard the interests of the Company in all their dealings and in the performance of their duties. They shall not expose the Company to any risk, whether financial or reputational.

2. SAFETY, SECURITY AND WELFARE

All employees, officers and directors of the Company should enjoy freedom from physical or emotional harm and should always feel safe at all times. Similarly, all employees should enjoy Security and Welfare i.e. freedom from the threat or fear of harm or danger. These could be achieved by starting the tasks from each employee and officers, by maintaining their work areas clean, secure and safe which shall ensure their health and safety and that of their co-employee as well.

3. HONESTY AND INTEGRITY

The most important traits in the workplace include honesty and integrity, that the employees, officers and directors are trustworthy, reliable, honest, truthful, upright and conscientious in the discharge of their functions, in the handling of company funds and properties, and in their dealings with their superiors, subordinates, fellow employees, customers, business partners and all other third parties. Any form of dishonesty or fraud is unacceptable and no employee, officer and director shall commit any such act nor conceal the same, facilitate or aid in the commission thereof.

4. RESPECT FOR SUPERIORS AND CO-EMPLOYEES

All employees, officers and directors shall observe courtesy and respect in dealing not only with their co-employees but with their superiors as well. They should exert cooperation and support for their specific work units and follow their superior’s assigned tasks to them.

5. RESPECT FOR CLIENTS/GUESTS/OTHER THIRD PARTIES

In line with the Company’s commitment to deliver excellent service to its clients, partners, business contacts, employees and other third parties and acknowledging that they are the lifeblood of our business, all employees, officers and directors are expected to treat them with the utmost importance and courtesy.

6. PROPER WORK CONDUCT AND BEHAVIOR / WORK-RELATED ETHICS

All employees, officers and directors are expected to observe proper decorum and conduct themselves in accordance with the values of Rampver Insure at all times within or outside of their work premises. They should realize that they are carrying the good name of Rampver Insure and hence, they are obliged to protect its reputation and image to ensure and maintain the confidence and trust of its clients and the public in general.

All employees, officers and directors shall perform their jobs or tasks with efficiency, conscientiousness and dedication and with the highest degree of professionalism, diligence, excellence and skill.


B. JURISDICTION

Except for minor offenses which shall be under the jurisdiction of the Human Resource Department, the Grievance Committee shall exercise jurisdiction over all violations of the provisions of this Code by employees and officers. It is composed of the President and Chief Executive Officer as the Chairman, and the MANCOM members consisting of the Head of Human Resource Department, the Head of the Compliance Department, the Head of the Operations Department, the Head of Marketing Department, the Head of Sales units, the Labor Relations Officer, and the Secretariat. A director who has committed an offense, shall be subject to the penalties to be imposed by the board of directors.
